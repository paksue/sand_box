07 / METHODOLOGYA MODEL, NOT A VERDICT

Principles are not proven by a score.

The numerical rankings are transparent editorial judgments intended to organize evidence, not validated measurements of character. High scores with limited evidence coverage are especially uncertain. Documented costly dissent and long-term opposition to conflicts of interest are weighted heavily.

No score proves personal corruption, bribery, hidden intent or a person's overall moral character. Unscored is not equivalent to untrustworthy.
