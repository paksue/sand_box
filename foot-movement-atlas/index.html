How a strong clinical team thinks

The site is organized around four questions: what moves, what controls the movement, what tolerates load, and what happens when you walk.

Mobility

Can the ankle, hip, spine and toes move comfortably without forcing or chasing pops?

Control

Can the foot tripod, knee and hip stay organized during balance, stepping and stance?

Capacity

Can calves, glutes, adductors, quads and trunk tolerate progressively more load?

Gait

Can you walk naturally without forefoot avoidance, toe-walking, guarding or next-day flare?

Important framing: this is a clinician-style organization of your project material, not a diagnosis or an individualized prescription. The green / yellow / red labels help organize discussion with your podiatrist or PT. Your painful medial-knee karate stance is kept as an assessment movement—not a stretch to force.
