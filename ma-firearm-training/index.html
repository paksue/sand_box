Simple plan for the three of you

If both friends are beginners

  • Start with Tactical Dynamics MA Gun Safety, Intro to Handguns, or the Danvers/Paladin beginner course.
  • Then take Mass Firearms Self Defense Law or a public GOAL law seminar together.

If they already handle handguns safely

  • Prioritize the dedicated law course.
  • Then consider a defensive-pistol course appropriate to their existing skill level and prerequisites.

Using your friend's friend

  • Useful for informal familiarization, range etiquette and helping beginners get comfortable.
  • Still use a qualified Massachusetts instructor or attorney-led class for authoritative legal instruction.

Massachusetts licensing-course change to remember

Massachusetts says Basic Firearms Safety courses conducted on or after April 2, 2026 must include safe handling/use/storage, secure storage/childproofing, applicable MA firearm laws, firearm operation, suicide/injury prevention, Massachusetts use-of-force law, disengagement/conflict avoidance, and live-fire training. When choosing an LTC/BFS course, verify that it is on the current approved-course list.
