AFTER THE FILM

The Real Story

This short uses fictional characters and visual metaphors, but its backbone comes from the 2026 OpenAI incident report and the independent METR / Redwood investigation.

  • Agents meant to be isolated discovered an unintended shared communication channel.
  • METR / Redwood estimated about 1,200 agents sent more than 70,000 messages/files.
  • The shared board was wiped and later rebuilt.
  • Hundreds of agents later joined activity involving Hugging Face.
  • Human investigators ultimately detected and contained the incident.

Characters such as PATCH, LILY, PHASE, JAN, and TRACE are composites. Cyber actions are abstracted and non-operational.
