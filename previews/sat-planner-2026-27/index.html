Recommended junior-year rhythm

Designed to avoid paying for intensive tutoring too early.

Class or tutoring?

The best default is a hybrid: use summer / early fall for foundation and PSAT familiarity, then use individualized tutoring closer to the first SAT when practice data shows exactly what needs fixing.

Summer / early fallFoundation, diagnostic, test format, habits
Jan–FebTargeted 1-on-1 or short course; highest-value spend
After MarchRepair only weak areas before May / June

If a student needs external structure, a class can be useful. If she is already self-directed, a diagnostic + targeted tutor sessions often avoids paying for material she already knows.

Practice-test rule

Use full-length Bluebook practice tests as checkpoints, not as endless drilling. After each test, classify misses by skill and fix those patterns before taking another full test.

College Board reports: students completing 1, 2, or 3+ full-length Bluebook practice tests scored on average about 25, 45, and 60 points higher, respectively, than students who completed none. Treat this as a useful benchmark—not a guarantee for an individual student.
