# WorldTap Product Constitution

## Mission

WorldTap is a fast, tactile daily game that answers one question:

> **You know the answer—but do you know where it is?**

The near-term business objective is intentionally small: prove that strangers will return, share, and eventually pay enough to reach **$100/month recurring revenue** without depending on paid advertising.

WorldTap does not need to become a blockbuster to succeed. It needs a small group of people who genuinely enjoy it and a tiny subset who pay for more.

## Core product promise

WorldTap should make geographic knowledge feel physical.

The player should:

1. see a target or clue,
2. spin Earth,
3. commit a location,
4. get an immediate distance/score reveal,
5. feel curiosity or surprise,
6. continue through five rounds,
7. share or return tomorrow.

The globe is not decoration. **The globe is the mechanic.**

## Primary audience

Start broad but design for people who already enjoy one or more of:

- geography and maps
- trivia
- Wordle-style daily games
- GeoGuessr-like spatial guessing
- travel, history, landmarks, flags, culture
- short games that fit into 1–3 minutes

Do not prematurely split the product into multiple personas or enterprise segments.

## Product priorities

In order:

1. **Core gameplay quality** — touching/spinning/guessing must feel excellent.
2. **Activation** — a new visitor understands the game and completes it.
3. **Retention** — a player voluntarily returns tomorrow and next week.
4. **Sharing** — results create a reason for another person to play.
5. **Monetization** — returning players can pay for meaningful extra value.
6. **Expansion** — new modes/features only after evidence supports them.

When priorities conflict, the earlier item wins unless the product owner explicitly decides otherwise.

## Free product

The following is a permanent product principle for the initial business model:

### Today's WorldTap is free.

A player should be able to arrive anonymously and complete the daily game without creating an account or paying.

Initial free experience:

- today's five-round game
- scoring and reveal
- streak
- basic local history/statistics
- result sharing
- challenge/return hook when available

The free daily game is both the product's promise and its acquisition surface.

## WorldTap+ initial value proposition

WorldTap+ should mean:

> **Play more. Learn more. See yourself improve.**

Do not launch with a sprawling bundle. The first paid version should center on three coherent benefits:

### 1. Archive

Play previous daily WorldTap games.

### 2. Unlimited themed games

Examples may include:

- capitals
- landmarks
- ancient world
- World War II
- sports
- food origins
- hard mode

Themes are examples, not commitments. Use player behavior to decide what to ship.

### 3. World Mastery

A visual learning/progress experience that shows where the player is strong or weak geographically and improves as they play.

Prefer deriving mastery from game-result data instead of creating a separate complex progression system until necessary.

## Pricing hypothesis

Initial test hypothesis, not a permanent rule:

- **$3.99/month**
- **$19.99/year**, presented as the primary choice

Pricing is a red-authority decision. Do not change prices automatically based on analytics without product-owner approval.

The first monetization milestone is not optimization. It is proof that a stranger will pay.

## Milestones

### Level 0 — Gameplay proof

The globe interaction, picking, reveal, scoring, and five-round flow feel trustworthy on the target phone.

### Level 1 — Stranger test

At least 10 people who are not building the product play it.

### Level 2 — Return test

At least 10 strangers voluntarily return for another day/session.

### Level 3 — Distribution test

100 people arrive without individually being invited by the product owner.

### Level 4 — Money test

At least one stranger pays at least $1 for WorldTap value.

### Level 5 — $10/month recurring

Validate the billing/entitlement/retention loop.

### Level 6 — $100/month recurring

This is the current business success target.

Only after this milestone should substantial Phase 2 social/product complexity become a default consideration.

## Core metrics

Keep the executive view small.

### Acquisition

- unique visitors / players
- source/channel where known

### Activation

- daily started
- daily completed
- start → completion rate

### Retention

- next-day return
- 7-day return
- streak distribution

### Virality

- result share rate
- challenge link created/opened when implemented

### Monetization

- Plus viewed
- checkout started
- checkout completed
- active paid subscribers
- MRR / normalized recurring revenue

Do not optimize vanity metrics if they do not connect to the loop above.

## Diagnostic rules

Use these before proposing new features:

- **Low traffic + strong retention:** distribution problem.
- **Traffic + low completion:** onboarding/gameplay problem.
- **Completion + low return:** retention/content problem.
- **Strong return + low Plus views:** merchandising/discovery problem.
- **Plus views + low checkout starts:** paid value proposition or price-framing problem.
- **Checkout starts + low purchases:** checkout/trust/price/payment problem.

Do not solve a distribution problem by adding product complexity, or a retention problem by buying traffic.

## Product experience principles

- Sessions should feel short enough to become habitual.
- The reveal moment should be emotionally stronger than the input form around it.
- Precision is the fun: being 18 km away should feel meaningfully different from being 1,800 km away.
- Content should broaden geography into culture/history/landmarks/etc. while preserving the central spatial mechanic.
- Sharing should express the player's performance and invite comparison—not merely advertise WorldTap.
- Account creation is a consequence of wanting more value, not an entrance requirement.

## Phase 1 non-goals

Do not build these merely because they are common in apps:

- public social network
- chat
- follower graph
- giant public leaderboard system
- classroom/teacher administration
- enterprise accounts
- native mobile apps
- ad network integration
- complex achievement economy
- marketplace/user-generated content platform
- real-time multiplayer
- microservices

Potentially valuable later:

- custom challenge links
- private friend/family leagues
- personalized learning/mastery

These require evidence, not enthusiasm.

## Feature admission test

Before implementing a substantial feature, answer:

1. What specific player problem or funnel metric does this address?
2. What is the smallest version that tests the hypothesis?
3. Can it be removed easily if it fails?
4. Does it preserve anonymous free daily play?
5. Does it preserve globe/touch performance?
6. What evidence will tell us it worked?

If those answers are weak, defer the feature.

## Business philosophy

WorldTap is being built as a **small profitable digital product first**.

The objective is not fundraising, headcount, technical prestige, or maximum feature surface. The objective is to learn the complete loop:

**find → try → love → return → pay → tell**

and make that loop work with real people.
