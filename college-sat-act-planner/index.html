04 / Find the right fit

SAT or ACT?

Neither exam is inherently better for admission. Compare *individual performance*, school requirements and preferred question style before paying for courses.

SAT / College Board

Focused + adaptive.

  • Digital adaptive test
  • 2 hours 14 minutes
  • Reading & Writing plus Math
  • Score: 400–1600
  • Shorter passages; no separate science section
  • Good choice if the question format feels natural
ACT / ACT, Inc.

More format choices.

  • Paper or digital; nonadaptive
  • 2 hours 5 minutes without optional add-ons
  • English, Math and Reading
  • Score: 1–36
  • Science and Writing are optional add-ons
  • Potential fit if students prefer its passage/test structure

06 / Protect your progress

Your data. Your control.

Back up your tracker

Export a JSON backup for another device or for safekeeping. On the other device, open this same site and import the JSON backup. Import replaces the data on that device; there is no automatic syncing.

Calendar alerts

Export all published SAT and ACT registration deadlines as calendar entries with advance reminders. Import into Apple or Google Calendar and confirm notifications are enabled. The webpage itself cannot push alerts when it is closed.
