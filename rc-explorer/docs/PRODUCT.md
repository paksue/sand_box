# Product

## Fantasy
Control a detailed 1:10-scale RC crawler in ordinary places that become enormous off-road worlds. The reward is tactile driving, scale, route-finding, discovery and atmosphere.

## First world — Backyard After Rain
Wet lawn, creek, seven-board bridge, rock garden, giant plants, deck/ramp, shed, drain tunnel, puddles and fence line.

## Player verbs
Drive, steer, brake, crawl, recover, switch camera, toggle lights, enter photo mode, discover.

## Session
No mandatory timer and no race. A satisfying session may be five minutes of free driving or twenty minutes finding a difficult route.

## Progress
Discoveries are persisted locally. Later worlds can add cosmetic bodies, tires, winch routes, photo goals and additional static chunks without adding a backend.

## Quality bar
The car must be enjoyable before objectives. The camera must make small terrain feel monumental. The first screen must read as a game, not a dashboard. 60 FPS is the desktop target with dynamic pixel ratio as protection.
