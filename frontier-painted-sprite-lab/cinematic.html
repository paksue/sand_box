The painting becomes a shot

This proof treats a trail moment like a directed movie scene, not a controllable character demo. Three.js supplies camera space and depth, GSAP supplies the shot timeline, and the existing painterly ox animation is consumed as one replaceable moving layer inside the stage.

Actor-rig benchmark Baseline lab
POC question: can a browser direct a painted scene like a film shot? The hero layer is intentionally swappable. Today it is generated by the previous PixiJS painted-ox rig; production could replace it with a Spine render, image sequence, transparent animation asset, or a fully pre-rendered event without changing the shot-direction model.

What this POC must prove

Composition: the shot has an intentional beginning, middle and settle—not an endlessly looping game scene.

Depth: foreground, actor, atmosphere and background separate when the camera moves.

Replaceability: the actor is merely an animated texture source. A higher-quality baked asset can replace it later.

Client only: everything runs from static HTML/JS assets.
