What must be true

Independent: buffalo and hero are real scene objects, not baked into the camera move.

Perspective: movement toward the horizon automatically reduces scale.

Depth: a farther actor can pass behind a nearer buffalo without special-case choreography.

Replaceable art: the movement/world code does not care whether the actor is an SVG stand-in, image sequence, or Spine rig.
