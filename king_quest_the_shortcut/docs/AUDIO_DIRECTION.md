# Audio Direction — The Moon Bell

Audio should sell a living fairy-tale world.

## Ambient beds
- Village: wet stone, distant hammering, cart wheels, post-storm drips.
- Mill: rushing river, wheel creak, wood strain, sluice roar.
- Briarwood: birds, leaves, distant wood knocks, shifting wind.
- Cottage: kettle, hanging herbs, hen clucks, garden insects.
- Chapel: open wind, ivy rustle, magpie calls, faint bell-metal resonance.
- Dusk: crickets, fireflies/moths, softer birds.
- Moonwell: quiet air, water/stone resonance, low magical tone.
- Brindle Bridge: ravine wind, bridge groan, troll snore before waking.
- Castle: distant guards, flag rope, stone echo, stronger wind.
- Bell Tower: exposed wind and then the full Moon Bell resonance.

## Music
Sparse, melodic, storybook.
Use themes at:
- opening quest;
- first entry into Briarwood;
- dusk transition;
- Moonwell success;
- castle approach;
- final bell and epilogue.

Do not wallpaper every screen.

## Interaction sounds
Keys, wood, rope, water, stone rings, lantern shutter, glass bottle, briar movement, gate latch, bell mechanism.

## Silence
Use silence before magical reveals and immediately before the Moon Bell rings.
