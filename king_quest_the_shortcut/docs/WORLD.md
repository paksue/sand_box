# World Bible — Ardenfall and the Briar Road

## Kingdom

**Ardenfall** is a small fairy-tale kingdom built around an old agreement between people and the living Briarwood.

Long ago, the first queen promised that once each Lantern Moon the castle's **Moon Bell** would be rung. In return, the Briarwood would keep its oldest paths open and its thorn spirits asleep around human settlements.

The ritual has become half ceremony, half forgotten infrastructure. Most villagers know only that the bell is "tradition."

The Bellkeeper knows better.

## The Sun Key

A palm-length brass-and-gold key with a sunburst bow.

It unlocks the Moon Bell's yoke. It is not a consumable puzzle item and cannot be permanently lost, traded, destroyed, or softlocked.

It also fits a few ancient courier locks along the Briar Road, subtly proving that road and ritual were once one system.

## Time structure

Start: **15:30**.
Important phases:
- 15:30–17:29: late afternoon.
- 17:30–18:29: golden hour; Ysabet gathers herbs for part of this interval.
- 18:30: dusk; ferry service begins; fireflies and moonmoths appear.
- 19:00: moonwell inscriptions become readable.
- 19:15: Brindle wakes.
- 20:30: moonrise; briars become more active; castle changes.
- 21:00: ninth evening bell / intended deadline.
- 21:00–22:00: late fail-forward state; the game remains completable, but routes and ending change.
- 22:00: no new long work; resolve through handover/final-state logic rather than free zero-time actions.

The clock should advance mainly through meaningful actions, travel, and explicit waiting. Reading/menus pause time.

## Locations

### L1 — Vale Locksmith & Village Lane
Warm late-afternoon starting scene. Half-open workshop, hanging keys, courier post, view toward the washed-out royal road.

Purpose:
- tutorial;
- receive Sun Key;
- collect a small starter set;
- learn that the main road is gone;
- glimpse the castle far away.

### L2 — Old Mill and Flooded Crossing
A rushing river, damaged footbridge, millwheel jammed with storm debris, sluice gate, spare beam, rope/capstan, lower ferry landing.

Purpose:
- first physical multi-solution puzzle;
- teach object/state interaction and time alternatives;
- establish Bram.

### L3 — Briar Crossroads
Three carved signposts around an old courier stone. Paths visibly loop. A stable old oak stands apart from the moving signs.

Purpose:
- navigation deduction;
- introduce the rule that the forest can move signs but not old boundary trees;
- first true "aha."

### L4 — Ysabet's Cottage and Herb Garden
Crooked green-roof cottage, drying herbs, copper kettle, lantern hook, hen coop, back window, moon-glass cabinet.

Purpose:
- NPC schedule;
- request/help/borrow/trespass alternatives;
- acquire or bypass True-Path light.

### L5 — Ruined Wayside Chapel
Roofless stone chapel swallowed by ivy. Small bell frame, faded courier mural, nesting magpie, silver hen occasionally hiding here.

Purpose:
- lore without exposition dump;
- clue for Moonwell;
- hen task;
- optional shiny trade / mirror acquisition;
- reveal old courier postern near castle.

### L6 — Whispering Hollow
A beautiful but deceptive stretch where paths fold and return. Sounds appear to come from the wrong direction.

Purpose:
- require True-Path lantern, glowjar, or late moonmoth route;
- make the magical navigation rule physical rather than menu-based.

### L7 — Moonwell Glade
Circular well, three stone moon-rings, reflective socket, pale flowers closed by day and open after dusk.

Purpose:
- KQIV-style revisit;
- moonlight/reflection puzzle;
- obtain Moonwater or alternate favor.

### L8 — Brindle's Moss Bridge
Stone bridge over a ravine. Brindle sleeps beneath it until 19:15. His brass gate key hangs near his nest. A thorn-choked toll bell is built into the bridge.

Purpose:
- schedule puzzle with help / stealth / alternate route;
- visible consequence and favor.

### L9 — Castle Approach and Postern
Moonlit hill, main gate, vine-hidden old courier door, distant Moon Bell tower.

Purpose:
- arrival route varies by time, proof, and earlier discoveries.

### L10 — Moon Bell Tower
Open stone belfry above the valley. Giant silver-dark bell, sun-shaped yoke lock, rope and briar growth whose severity depends on time/state.

Purpose:
- final culmination;
- use Sun Key;
- earlier preparation changes final difficulty;
- ending vista.

## Travel topology

Village → Mill → Crossroads.

From Crossroads:
- Cottage
- Chapel
- deeper Briar Road / Hollow

Hollow → Moonwell → Brindle Bridge → Castle.

Later clues can reveal bypasses:
- dusk ferry bypasses Mill repair;
- moonmoths can bypass the Crossroads/Hollow deduction after dusk;
- True-Path light reveals under-bridge or castle-postern details.

## Magic rules

Magic must feel rule-bound and observable.

1. **Briarwood may move signs and paths, but ancient boundary stones/trees remain fixed.**
2. **True-Path light reveals edges, inscriptions, and routes concealed by glamour.**
3. **Moonwater loosens living briar and wakes certain old stone enchantments.**
4. **Moonflowers and moonwell inscriptions react only after dusk.**
5. **Old courier locks recognize the Sun Key.**
6. Magic never solves unrelated problems merely because it is magical.

## Tone

Wondrous, slightly eerie, playful, humane.

Danger exists, but the POC does not need combat. Humor should come from character, fairy-tale logic, and object responses rather than modern snark.
