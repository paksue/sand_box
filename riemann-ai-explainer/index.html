How should I interpret these diagrams?

These are teaching illustrations, not plots of the paper's raw data or a reproduction of the Lean proof. The Möbius example uses real calculated values; the transformed waves and hypothetical contradiction are conceptual analogies.

Important: OpenAI's 7/8 theorem is a published, Lean-formalized mathematical claim. The complete Riemann Hypothesis remains unproved.

🔎 Evidence & context

OpenAI's main manuscript states that no nontrivial zero of the zeta function has real part greater than 7/8. Symmetry rules out real parts below 1/8. This is weaker than requiring every zero to lie exactly on 1/2.
