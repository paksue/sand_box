# The Shortcut: The Moon Bell

An original browser-based fantasy graphical adventure inspired by the design craft of classic Sierra adventures, especially King's Quest III–VI.

## Creative direction
The project is now a **fantasy reboot**. The earlier office prototype ("The Conference") is historical only.

Mara Vale, a locksmith's apprentice, must carry the Sun Key through the enchanted Briar Road and reach Castle Arden before the Moon Bell's ninth evening bell.

Useful engine foundations from the old prototype may be retained; office content should not.
