# Credits and licensing for generated narration

The *Midnight Japanese* lesson text was written for this app. Its generated
male voice clips do **not** contain any Midnight Diner television audio, actor
voice samples, or actor impersonation training data.

Audio model: [Kokoro-82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M)
by hexgrad and contributors; model weights published under Apache 2.0.
Voice: \`jm_kumo\` (Japanese, male), supplied with Kokoro.
This voice is derived from Koniwa's \`tnc__kumonoito\` / Television Nishinippon
Japanese literary readings, whose source recordings require Creative Commons
Attribution 3.0 credit according to Kokoro voice provenance.

Attribution: "Koniwa / Television Nishinippon — \`tnc__kumonoito\`
source audio (CC BY 3.0), via Kokoro \`jm_kumo\` Japanese voice."

The final generated speech is synthetic and can contain incorrect readings.
Review it for language accuracy before treating it as an authoritative
pronunciation source. The user's own uploaded clip always overrides the
generated voice for that phrase.
