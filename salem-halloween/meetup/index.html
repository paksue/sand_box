If something changes.

Short instructions for both sides of the plan.

👭 For the girls

  1. Stay together. No one goes off alone.
  2. After the show, text Dad your exact street corner and share live location.
  3. Wait in a well-lit public area with staff or police nearby. Dad comes to you.
  4. Lost or separated? Call each other and Dad. If no phone, ask staff/police for help; use 95 Margin St only when safe.

🚗 For Dad

  1. Margin St is the first drop-off option. If blocked, use accessible legal stopping/parking, such as 108 Jefferson Ave if space is available.
  2. Try Jefferson Ave parking before the drone show; if full, find another legal space.
  3. Message the updated parking location; meet girls on foot rather than having them search for the car.
  4. Don't drive onto barricaded downtown roads or depend on the Salem MBTA garage.
🚨 Immediate danger? Call 911.

If unsafe to speak, text 911 in Massachusetts. Go toward police or staffed public places.
