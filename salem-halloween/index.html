01 / GO & RETURN

A good night has an exit plan.

The highest-leverage parental help: make the ride home predictable, without shadowing the group.

BEFORE YOU GO

Train in. Backup arranged.

Go together via North Station → Salem. Check MBTA alerts and save an earlier return option. City lists Boston's last outbound Salem train at 11:14 PM for Oct 31. 2026 guide ↗

CROWD LOGISTICS

Don't plan a downtown car pickup.

Major streets close by noon. Garage access is restricted; city notices differ on the exact reopening time. Arrange pickup outside closures at a mutually verified location. Current rules ↗

ALERTS

Get the city's warnings.

Text join SALEMALERT to 58339 to subscribe to Salem emergency notifications. Also enable transit alerts. Official enrollment ↗

02B / THE HIDDEN NUMBER

What police counts cannot see.

National victim surveys measure harms absent from police records. They do not establish a Salem Halloween rate.

EVIDENCE METHOD / READ FIRST

Two arrests never meant two victims.

Arrests, recorded incidents, victim experiences and convictions are four different things. Some crimes are not reported; some reports do not lead to arrests. Never divide Halloween arrests by the visitor count and call it the risk of assault.

ABDUCTION / DIFFERENT DATA

Stranger abduction is rare, but attempts deserve a practiced response.

Nonfamily abductions were 1% of missing-child cases reported to NCMEC; this is not a 1% risk per child, a measure of all abduction attempts, or a Salem estimate. NCMEC's attempted-abduction research documents children escaping by refusing, making noise, using a phone and attracting intervention.

ADAPT THE PLAN

Four actions that matter more than the unknown count.

01Unwanted touching / groping

Move immediately to friends and identifiable staff. Tell someone clearly what happened. Calling police is an option; urgent threat → 911.

02Followed / grabbed / coerced

Move toward people and staffed areas; shout for help if restrained, resist being taken away, and call 911. Do not follow a stranger to a car or secluded area.

03Friend seems drugged / incapacitated

Stay with them, get event staff or emergency medical help, and tell responders about suspected drink tampering. Don't leave them with strangers.

04Possible sexual assault

Get safe and seek emergency medical care promptly. Massachusetts permits forensic evidence collection up to 120 hours, even without choosing to report to police.

Where to get confidential help: Massachusetts rape crisis centers provide free services to adolescents. MA survivor options ↗ · Local crisis centers ↗ · BARCC 24/7: 800-841-8371. For immediate danger call 911.

Data note: NCVS 2024 estimates are national, for people age 12+; reporting percentages are rounded. The public-space survey is adults 18+ recalling lifetime experiences. Reports of attempted abduction are incomplete and differ in definition from NCMEC missing-child case types. We found no valid scientific basis to assign Salem Halloween-specific unreported counts for 2016–2025.

04 / LIGHTWEIGHT BACKUPS

Gear that earns its weight.

No device can guarantee safety. Prioritize reliability and ease of use over tactical marketing claims.

Walkie-talkie limitation: short-range FRS radios can help group members reach each other if phone networks struggle; they cannot summon police and may fail among dense buildings. Test the exact route first. No realistic reason to carry a satellite messenger just for downtown Salem.
