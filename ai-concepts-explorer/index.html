Training changes weights.

These approaches work together, but solve different problems. Learning the difference is one of the fastest ways to make sense of AI products and architecture.

EXPERIMENT 04 / NEXT-TOKEN PREDICTION

Why isn't every answer identical?

A model produces scores for possible next tokens. Temperature and sampling filters transform those scores into a selection distribution. Try changing the controls, then sample an answer.

This demonstration starts with fixed, invented logits. Top-K is applied first, then Top-P to the probability distribution over remaining candidates. The last probabilities are normalized before sampling.

What to notice: lower temperature concentrates probabilities. It does not make a model more truthful; it only changes how candidates are selected.
