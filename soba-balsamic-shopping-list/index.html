Kitchen notes · 03

Make your own tsuyu.

Authentic-style cold soba dipping sauce. No added preservatives, MSG or artificial flavoring required.

The homemade route

Slow-steeped.
Deeply savory.

A simple recipe using brewed soy sauce, real mirin, kombu and dried bonito. Refrigerate for 3–4 days or freeze in small portions.

5 minActive prep
20–30Soak · min
10–15Cook · min

Ingredients

Servings4

Adjust portions with the serving controls. Check ingredients as you go.

Five simple steps

  1. Soak kombu in water for 20–30 minutes.
  2. Heat gently and remove kombu just before boiling.
  3. Add soy sauce, mirin and optional sugar. Simmer 2–3 minutes.
  4. Add bonito flakes, simmer gently for one minute, then turn off heat.
  5. Steep 5 minutes, strain, cool promptly and refrigerate. Serve chilled with soba.
